Pigeon Payroll

I want to create a comedic mobile arcade game about a cafe worker who must keep a flock of pigeons away from outdoor customers. The player uses one-finger swipes to throw breadcrumbs and herd the flock, but every breadcrumb also attracts more pigeons and adds to a cleanup bill deducted from the final score. Each shift lasts roughly 60 to 90 seconds and becomes more chaotic as customers move tables, pastries appear, and impatient birds form combos by stealing food. The tone should be playful rather than stressful, with bold readable characters, slapstick sound effects, and portrait-mode play. A first prototype only needs one cafe screen, three customer tables, and a small set of pigeon behaviours. I am unsure whether the best audience is children, casual adults, or both, and whether failure should end a run or simply reduce the player's wages.
