Borrowed Orbit

I want a minimalist one-button browser game where a small satellite survives by borrowing gravity from objects moving across the screen. Holding the button attaches the satellite to the nearest planet or piece of debris; releasing it preserves momentum and sends the player into a new arc. The goal is to collect data fragments, skim hazards for bonus points, and keep an orbit multiplier alive while the available objects drift toward a collapsing star. Runs should take less than three minutes, restart instantly, and be understandable from animation and sound instead of text. The visual style should be sparse geometric space art that a solo developer could produce. The prototype should prove that switching gravity anchors creates skillful, predictable movement on one screen. I have not chosen the exact physics model, input accessibility features, target frame rate on low-end devices, or how much randomness each run should contain.
