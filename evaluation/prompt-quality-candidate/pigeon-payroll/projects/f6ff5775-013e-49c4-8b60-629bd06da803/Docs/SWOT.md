# SWOT

This initiation SWOT separates established project facts from planning inference. It is not market validation, a success claim, or a product-positioning decision.

## Strengths

| Observation | Classification | Trace | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breadcrumbs both herd pigeons and create attraction and score costs. | Established brief fact | REQ-005; REQ-006; REQ-007 | The core action has an explicit trade-off suitable for prototype observation. |
| The prototype scope names one cafe screen and three tables. | Established brief constraint | REQ-017; REQ-018 | Scene scope is bounded even though behaviour count is not. |
| Shift duration and portrait orientation are stated. | Established brief constraint | REQ-008; REQ-016 | Session and layout verification have clear outer bounds. |
| Playful tone, readable characters, and slapstick audio align around a coherent presentation goal. | Design inference from established requirements | REQ-013; REQ-014; REQ-015 | Identity work can explore a consistent direction, subject to stakeholder review. |

## Weaknesses

| Observation | Classification | Trace | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intended audience is unselected. | Established unresolved choice | OQ-001; DEC-020 | Positioning, evaluation participants, and child-related platform scope cannot be finalized. |
| Failure handling is unselected. | Established unresolved choice | OQ-002; DEC-021 | A complete run-state and score-economy specification is blocked. |
| “Small” does not define a numeric behaviour limit or full transition set. | Established ambiguity | REQ-019; OQ-003 | Behaviour implementation beyond mandatory actions requires approval. |
| Prototype success has no approved measures or thresholds. | Established unresolved choice | OQ-004; DEC-022 | The team may test requirements, but cannot honestly declare prototype success. |

## Opportunities

| Observation | Classification | Trace | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A deterministic harness could expose whether the trade-off and escalation are behaving as specified. | Engineering inference | A-004; VER-004; VER-005 | Build-only instrumentation may reduce ambiguity during iteration. |
| Failure-rule variants could be tested after the stakeholder authorizes variants and evaluation criteria. | Recommendation, not decision | OQ-002; OQ-004; EVD-005; EVD-006; EVD-007 | A comparison can generate evidence without treating research as product authority. |
| Additional pigeon behaviours could draw inspiration from the behaviour evidence after scope approval. | Design inference, not decision | OQ-003; EVD-008; EVD-009; EVD-010 | Plausibility is available as inspiration, but suitability remains unproven. |

## Threats

| Observation | Classification | Trace | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience-dependent platform and privacy risks are documented in the canonical evidence records. | Research-constrained risk | OQ-001; EVD-001; EVD-002; EVD-003; EVD-004 | Do not add ads, analytics, accounts, or audience declarations before an authorized decision and compliance review. |
| Rising chaos can conflict with playful tone and readability if tuning is not defined. | Design inference | REQ-009; REQ-013; REQ-014; OQ-006 | Treat pressure values as hypotheses and evaluate them under approved criteria. |
| An engine choice made before platform, device, budget, and team constraints are known may create rework. | Delivery inference | A-002; OQ-005 | Keep the initiation architecture logical and technology-neutral. |
