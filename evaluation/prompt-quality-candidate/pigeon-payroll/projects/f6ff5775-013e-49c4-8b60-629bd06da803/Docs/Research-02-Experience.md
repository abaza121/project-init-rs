# Research 02 — experience and pigeon behaviour

Research questions: What evidence can inform failure feedback, challenge, and plausible pigeon behaviour?  
Outcome: **The evidence provides constraints and inspiration, not authority to choose the failure rule or complete behaviour set. OQ-002 and OQ-003 remain open.**  
Verification date: 2026-08-30.

This file is the canonical location for the retained experience and behaviour evidence claims.

## Failure and enjoyment evidence

### EVD-005

**Sourced fact.** A mixed-methods study of 182 participants found that success was generally more enjoyable than failure, while challenge-oriented players were more likely to experience competence and enjoyment during failure; it did not compare wage deductions with early run termination. [Utrecht University Research Portal — The Struggle is Spiel: On Failure and Success in Games](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/the-struggle-is-spiel-on-failure-and-success-in-games/)

- Reliability: high; primary peer-reviewed research record, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: no direct comparison of the two Pigeon Payroll candidates.
- Informs: DEC-021 and OQ-002.

### EVD-006

**Sourced fact.** Research using three large video-game datasets and two controlled experiments found preferences for intermediate difficulty, outcomes matching expected difficulty, and success; it did not prescribe a failure mechanic for this prototype. [Scientific Reports — Leveling up fun: learning progress, expectations, and success influence enjoyment in video games](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-14628-2)

- Reliability: high; primary research, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: general enjoyment evidence does not select a project-specific failure rule or tuning value.
- Informs: DEC-021 and OQ-002.

### EVD-007

**Sourced fact.** In a 77-participant educational interactive-narrative study, participants identified harsh, vague, or confusing feedback as reducing enjoyment, and some disliked returning to the beginning after a bad ending. [Frontiers in Virtual Reality — Using Self-Determination Theory to Explore Enjoyment of Educational Interactive Narrative Games](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/virtual-reality/articles/10.3389/frvir.2022.847120/full)

- Reliability: medium; primary but context-specific research, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: applying an educational narrative result to a short arcade game is a design inference.
- Informs: DEC-021 and OQ-002.

## Pigeon-behaviour evidence

### EVD-008

**Sourced fact.** An experiment found that urban feral pigeons used food supplied by people and learned within six to nine sessions to avoid a feeder who had previously chased them away. [PubMed — Pigeons discriminate between human feeders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21647649/)

- Reliability: high; primary behavioural research abstract, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: plausible natural behaviour is not proof of readable, comic, or scoped game behaviour.
- Informs: DEC-019 and OQ-003.

### EVD-009

**Imported sourced fact.** A high-resolution GPS study of homing-pigeon pairs found alignment with a neighbour and attraction or avoidance responses that depended on distance. [PubMed Central — Interaction rules underlying group decisions in homing pigeons](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3808543/)

- Reliability: high in the supplied snapshot; primary research.
- Availability: the linked page presented an automated-access challenge on 2026-08-30, so the claim was not independently re-verified in this session.
- Boundary: applying airborne pair behaviour to a cafe game is a design inference.
- Informs: DEC-019 and OQ-003.

### EVD-010

**Imported sourced fact.** An experiment with pigeon flocks found that observers learned a food-access response from a trained pigeon and adopted producer and scrounger roles. [PubMed — Social learning of optimal responding and producer-scrounger strategies in flocks of pigeons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17296109/)

- Reliability: high in the supplied snapshot; primary research.
- Availability: the linked record returned an internal retrieval error on 2026-08-30, so the claim was not independently re-verified in this session.
- Boundary: the result does not identify a suitable prototype behaviour or transition.
- Informs: DEC-019 and OQ-003.

## Design inferences and recommendation boundary

- **Design inference:** recoverable or non-harsh failure feedback may align with REQ-013, but it is not an approved failure rule.
- **Design inference:** learned avoidance or simple local coordination may inspire variants, but they are not approved additions to the behaviour registry.
- **Recommendation:** once OQ-002 and OQ-004 are authorized, compare labelled failure variants under approved criteria. This is not a decision.
- **Tuning hypothesis:** none of these sources establishes deduction size, attraction range, impatience threshold, flocking weight, or combo timing.
