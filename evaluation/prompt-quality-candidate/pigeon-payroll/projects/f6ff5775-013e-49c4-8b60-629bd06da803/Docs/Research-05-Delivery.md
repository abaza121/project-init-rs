# Research 05 — delivery and evaluation

Research question: What general evidence constrains the definition of prototype success?  
Outcome: **General evaluation frameworks support measurable stakeholder-owned criteria but do not supply Pigeon Payroll thresholds. OQ-004 remains open.**  
Verification date: 2026-08-30.

This file is the canonical location for delivery and evaluation evidence claims.

## Canonical evidence

### EVD-011

**Sourced fact.** ISO 9241-11:2018 provides a framework for understanding usability as an outcome of use and applying that concept in design and evaluation, but its official summary does not supply project-specific success criteria. [ISO — ISO 9241-11:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html)

- Reliability: high; official standards-body summary, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: it does not choose a Pigeon Payroll method, participant group, measure, or threshold.
- Informs: DEC-022 and OQ-004.

### EVD-012

**Sourced fact.** GOV.UK usability guidance identifies measures such as task completion, completion time, abandonment, perceived ease, and confidence, while requiring teams to establish the relevant research scope. [GOV.UK Service Manual — Usability benchmarking a website or whole service](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/usability-benchmarking-a-website-or-whole-service)

- Reliability: high; authoritative service guidance, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: applying service benchmarking measures to a mobile arcade game is an inference; no game-specific threshold is supplied.
- Informs: DEC-022 and OQ-004.

### EVD-013

**Sourced fact.** NASA systems-engineering guidance states that objectives should have measurable, quantifiable, verifiable, and feasible target levels, and that measures of effectiveness correspond to stakeholder expectations and project-success criteria. [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook — Stakeholder Expectations Definition](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-1-stakeholder-expectations-definition/)

- Reliability: high; authoritative general systems-engineering guidance, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: it establishes no game objective, measure, participant count, or threshold.
- Informs: DEC-022 and REQ-023.

## Delivery consequence

ANS-004 correctly failed to manufacture success criteria. VER-006 must report **not evaluated** until OQ-004 authorizes the relevant perceptual protocol, and VER-011 prevents a prototype-success declaration until approved measures and pass/fail thresholds have corresponding recorded results.

No participant count, task-completion target, satisfaction score, performance budget, retention target, schedule, cost, or defect threshold is established.
