# Research 01 — audience

Research question: What external constraints matter if the stakeholder selects children, casual adults, or both?  
Outcome: **Research constrains consequences but does not select an audience. OQ-001 remains open.**  
Verification date: 2026-08-30.

This file is the canonical location for audience evidence claims. Other documents reference the evidence IDs rather than restating these claims.

## Canonical evidence

### EVD-001

**Sourced fact.** Google Play instructs developers to select both child and adult age groups only if the app was designed for, and is appropriate for, every selected group; selecting any child age group invokes its Families Policy Requirements. [Google Play Console Help — Manage target audience and app content settings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en-EN)

- Reliability: high; first-party platform guidance, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: this constrains declarations; it does not reveal stakeholder intent.
- Informs: DEC-020 and OQ-001.

### EVD-002

**Sourced fact.** Google Play imposes additional data-handling requirements for apps targeting both children and older users, including protections for children and users of unknown age; some SDK use may require a neutral age screen or equivalent controls. [Google Play Console Help — Data practices in Families apps](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11043825?hl=en)

- Reliability: high; first-party platform guidance, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: this shows consequences of a mixed audience; it does not support choosing one.
- Informs: DEC-020 and OQ-008.

### EVD-003

**Sourced fact.** The FTC lists intended audience and empirical audience composition among the factors for determining whether an online service is child-directed and defines a mixed audience as child-directed content in which children under 13 are targeted but are not the primary audience. [Federal Trade Commission — Complying with COPPA: Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)

- Reliability: high; authoritative US regulator guidance, independently opened on 2026-08-30. The page notes the rule was amended in 2025 and distinguishes staff guidance from binding Commission action.
- Boundary: legal applicability depends on facts and jurisdiction; this package is not legal advice and the source supplies no project intent.
- Informs: DEC-020 and OQ-001.

### EVD-004

**Sourced fact.** Apple describes its Kids category as intended for apps designed for children and requires age-appropriate experiences, protection of children's data, and parental gates for certain actions. [Apple Developer — Design safe and age-appropriate experiences](https://developer.apple.com/kids/)

- Reliability: high; first-party platform guidance, independently opened on 2026-08-30.
- Boundary: this shows consequences of selecting a child audience; it does not establish that Pigeon Payroll should select one.
- Informs: DEC-020 and OQ-001.

## Evidence-based conclusion

**Authority boundary:** No retained evidence provides stakeholder intent. ANS-001 correctly records a failed attempt to settle the product-positioning choice. REQ-020 therefore requires the audience to remain unresolved until an authorized answer closes OQ-001.
