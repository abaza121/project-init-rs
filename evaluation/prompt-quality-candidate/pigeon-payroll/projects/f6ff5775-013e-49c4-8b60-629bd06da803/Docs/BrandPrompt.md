# Brand prompt

Status: **proposed concept-art prompt based on A-006; not approval to produce final marketing assets**.

## Prompt

Create a portrait mobile-game key-art and gameplay-style exploration for **“Pigeon Payroll.”** Show one compact outdoor cafe screen with exactly three customer tables. A bold, readable cafe worker uses a sweeping one-finger gesture to toss a curved trail of golden breadcrumbs, herding a flock away from customers. Make the trade-off visible: more pigeons are arriving toward the crumbs while a comic cleanup-bill counter rises. Include a pastry theft and a clear combo reaction as background slapstick. Use large silhouettes, rounded shapes, expressive poses, thick dark outlines, simple cafe geometry, and generous negative space for mobile readability. Aim for playful, kinetic comedy rather than panic or menace. Suggested exploratory colors: warm cream ground, charcoal outlines, teal worker accents, tomato reactions, mustard crumbs, blue-grey pigeons, and purple combo accents. Leave space for the title and compact score, time, and cleanup UI.

Avoid: landscape composition; extra cafe screens; more or fewer than three tables; photorealism; frightening aggression; visual clutter; tiny facial details; gore; humiliation; text implying a child, adult, or all-ages audience; a depicted early game-over or wage-only failure rule; any claim that concept art is final brand approval.
