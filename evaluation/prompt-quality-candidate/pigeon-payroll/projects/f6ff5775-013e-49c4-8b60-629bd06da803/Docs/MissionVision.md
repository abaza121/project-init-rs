# Mission and vision

Authority status: **proposed internal framing under A-007; not stakeholder-approved public copy**.

## Mission

Create a short, readable mobile arcade prototype in which one-finger breadcrumb throws turn pigeon herding into a comic risk-and-reward loop: every useful lure also increases flock pressure and the cleanup deduction.

This proposed summary is derived from BCL-002, BCL-003, BCL-004, and BCL-005. It does not resolve audience, failure, behaviour scope, or success criteria.

## Vision

Make each cafe shift feel like a compact slapstick story whose chaos rises while the player can still understand what happened, act with one finger, and see how their choices affected the final score.

This proposed summary is derived from REQ-004, REQ-007, REQ-008, REQ-009, REQ-013, REQ-014, and REQ-015. “Understand” is an intended quality, not a passed outcome; OQ-004 must define its evaluation.

## Prototype principles

| Principle | Authority | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Useful actions carry comic cost. | Brief-derived: REQ-005, REQ-006, REQ-007 | Present attraction pressure and cleanup cost as legible consequences of breadcrumb use. |
| Escalate without abandoning readability. | Brief-derived plus proposed interpretation: REQ-009, REQ-014, A-005 | Increase event pressure while preserving distinct characters, targets, and feedback. |
| Finish the smallest honest test. | Brief-derived: REQ-017, REQ-018, REQ-019 | Keep one cafe screen and three tables; do not fill unresolved behaviour scope by invention. |
| Keep product authority visible. | Governance: REQ-020, REQ-021, REQ-022, REQ-023 | Mark unresolved choices instead of presenting recommendations as decisions. |
