# Visual identity

Status: **prototype identity direction; proposed under A-006 and not a final brand system**.

## Authoritative anchors

- REQ-013 requires a playful rather than stressful tone.
- REQ-014 requires bold, readable characters.
- REQ-015 requires slapstick sound effects.
- REQ-016 requires portrait play.
- REQ-017 constrains the prototype to one cafe gameplay screen.

Audience-coded art choices are deliberately deferred under OQ-001.

## Proposed visual system

| Element | Proposed direction | Reason and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Shape language | Large silhouettes, rounded bodies, oversized feet and beaks, simple table geometry. | Inference intended to support REQ-014; readability must still be evaluated under OQ-004. |
| Composition | Three table anchors in a portrait playfield, clear pedestrian paths, uncluttered score and cleanup areas. | Applies REQ-016 and REQ-018; exact layout is not approved. |
| Motion | Short anticipation, quick impact, soft overshoot, and visible recovery for throws, hops, steals, and reactions. | Proposed slapstick treatment supporting REQ-013 and REQ-015; timing is tuning. |
| Iconography | Breadcrumb, bill, pastry, impatience, and combo symbols with distinct silhouettes and redundant shape cues. | Proposed readability aid; no accessibility standard is claimed. |
| Typography | Heavy display face for the title; plain, open counters for score, time, and cleanup bill. | Proposed interpretation of “bold readable”; font selection and licensing remain open. |

## Character distinction

- Cafe worker: upright silhouette, apron block, teal action accent.
- Customers: seated or moving silhouettes with table-linked color markers.
- Pigeons: low rounded silhouette; state changes use pose and icon as well as color.
- Impatient pigeon: repeated foot motion, forward lean, and impatience icon; do not rely on color alone.

These are design proposals. The exact character roster, representation, and audience fit require stakeholder approval.

## Audio identity boundary

Visual identity should leave room for visible anticipation and impact that synchronize with the distinct slapstick cues required by AC-015. No sound assets, composer direction, loudness targets, or haptic requirements have been authorized.
