# Research 03 — Market and Positioning

## Scope

This memo asks what market claims can responsibly be made from the available authority and retained evidence.

## Supported conclusion

Only the PC target and 2D score-attack concept are stakeholder-authorized (`SRC-002`). The intended audience remains open (`ANS-004`), and `EVD-009` establishes only that a content rating has a different purpose from target-audience selection. Therefore no market segment, competitor set, commercial model, demand estimate, price, storefront, territory, or launch strategy is baselined.

## Decision impact

- `D-010` keeps intended audience open.
- `DAD-REQ-003` retains PC while deferring the detailed support baseline to `OQ-009`.
- `OQ-014` retains persistence, leaderboards, and online-service scope as unresolved rather than treating “score-attack” as authority for them.

## Research availability

Market sizing, comparable-product analysis, pricing research, storefront review, and demand validation are unavailable because no geography, audience, commercial goal, budget, or approved comparable set was supplied. Broad web search would produce decorative claims that cannot yet inform a requirement or authorized decision, so none is retained.

## Next research gate

After stakeholders answer `OQ-002`, `OQ-006`, `OQ-009`, `OQ-014`, and `OQ-015`, a market brief can define a research population, territories, comparable-selection criteria, and decision questions. Until then, positioning language in [MissionVision.md](MissionVision.md) and [VisualIdentity.md](VisualIdentity.md) is explicitly proposed, not market-validated.
