# Research 05 — Delivery and Governance

## Research question

What external guidance supports leaving preference-bearing, scope, and acceptance choices with stakeholders?

## Canonical evidence records

### EVD-007 — Weights encode relative preference

- Claim: In a linear additive model, criterion weights indicate relative strength of preference, are elicited from decision stakeholders, and materially affect overall value.
- Source: [UK Government Analysis Function — An Introductory Guide to Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/an-introductory-guide-to-mcda/)
- Reliability: high; primary government methodological guidance.
- Verified: 2026-08-30.
- Authority boundary: supports stakeholder ownership of weights; does not prescribe scoring weights for this game.
- Trace: `DAD-REQ-012`; `D-009`; `OQ-003`.

### EVD-011 — Excluded work should be agreed

- Claim: UK government project-management guidance says project scope should identify what is in and out and that specifically excluded work should be defined and agreed by interested parties.
- Source: [Guidelines for Managing Projects: How to organise, plan and control projects (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a790afced915d0422067576/10-1257-guidelines-for-managing-projects.pdf)
- Reliability: high for general project governance; primary government publication, though dated.
- Verified: 2026-08-30.
- Authority boundary: supports agreement on exclusions; does not identify any exclusion for Dead Air Dispatch.
- Trace: `DAD-REQ-018`; `D-012`; `OQ-006`.

### EVD-012 — Stakeholder expectations remain provisional until agreement

- Claim: NASA systems-engineering guidance says stakeholders should agree on the problem and scope, and initial stakeholder expectations remain provisional until refined and finally agreed.
- Source: [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook — Stakeholder Expectations Definition](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-1-stakeholder-expectations-definition/)
- Reliability: high for stakeholder-expectation governance; primary agency handbook.
- Verified: 2026-08-30.
- Authority boundary: supports the authority and baselining process; does not supply project-specific scope.
- Trace: `DAD-REQ-018`; `D-012`; `OQ-006`; `OQ-015`.

## Synthesis

- Evidence: score weights express preference, so the three values must not be invented by research.
- Evidence: exclusions and expectation baselines require interested-party/stakeholder agreement.
- Inference/recommendation: use the approvals listed in `OQ-015` and baseline decisions individually. This is a delivery recommendation, not proof that a schedule, owner, or budget exists.
