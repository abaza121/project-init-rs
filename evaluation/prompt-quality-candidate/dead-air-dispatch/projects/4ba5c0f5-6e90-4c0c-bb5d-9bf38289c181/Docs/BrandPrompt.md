# Brand Prompt

Status: reusable creative prompt based on the unapproved proposal in [VisualIdentity.md](VisualIdentity.md). It must not be treated as final brand authority.

## Primary prompt

Design a key visual and compact identity exploration for **Dead Air Dispatch**, a strange 2D PC score-attack game about operating an emergency radio during a supernatural storm. Frame the image as a single nocturnal radio room seen through an instrument-panel composition. Make a physical or graphic tuning dial the focal point. Surround it with abstract waveforms, distant stranded-caller silhouettes, broken safe-route marks, antenna geometry, and hostile interference closing in through bands of restrained static. The mood is urgent, uncanny, lonely, and precise—not military, comic, or nostalgic parody.

Use a storm-black and receiver-navy field with signal-ivory information, rescue-amber caller emphasis, hazard-magenta interference, and route-cyan navigation accents. Communicate different signal states through shape, pattern, motion implication, labels, and spacing as well as color. Keep critical information crisp above the texture. Favor narrow instrument marks, disciplined grid spacing, a condensed display face for the title, and a highly legible interface face for small information. Explore an uppercase or small-cap **DEAD AIR** wordmark with wider tracking and **DISPATCH** as a compact stamped subline, optionally crossed by a tuning notch or interrupted carrier line.

Deliver a clean key art composition, wordmark study, small palette strip, and five signal-state motifs: caller present, signal locked, safe route, dangerous band, and hostile approach. Treat captions, visual audio equivalents, mono, and audio controls as unselected accessibility candidates; do not depict them as shipped features.

## Negative prompt / exclusions for this creative exercise

These are prompt guardrails, not project-scope exclusions under `D-012`.

Avoid photoreal people, literal disaster-response branding, real government seals, generic cyberpunk neon, excessive VHS nostalgia, illegible glitch typography, color-only state coding, busy multi-room scenes, weapons, gore, comedy-horror, mobile-game UI, leaderboards, online-service badges, platform storefront logos, and any accessibility certification claim.
