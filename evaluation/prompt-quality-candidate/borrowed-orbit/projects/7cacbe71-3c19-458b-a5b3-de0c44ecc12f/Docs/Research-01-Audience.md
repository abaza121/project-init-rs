# Research 01 — Audience

## Research question

Who is Borrowed Orbit for, and which input/accessibility contexts must the prototype represent?

## Evidence retained

The canonical evidence records are [EVD-004](Traceability.md#evd-004), [EVD-005](Traceability.md#evd-005), [EVD-006](Traceability.md#evd-006), and [EVD-013](Traceability.md#evd-013). Their external source links and claim wording live only in the evidence register.

## Evidence-grounded interpretation

- EVD-013 supports learning likely users and goals through project-specific research and treating unsupported audience ideas as hypotheses. It informs D-012 and OQ-008; it does not identify an audience.
- EVD-004, EVD-005, and EVD-006 support considering keyboard operation, concurrent input modalities, and alternatives to sustained input. They inform D-009 and D-014; they do not authorize the project’s modalities, bindings, remapping, or Toggle mode.

## Inference, explicitly non-authoritative

The brief’s one-button mechanic, short runs, minimalist presentation, and browser format could suit several different player groups. Those traits do not establish age, prior game experience, ability profile, device context, or motivation. No audience segment is adopted in this package.

A responsible discovery activity would recruit against stakeholder-approved hypotheses and observe first-run understanding, hold/release operation, trajectory prediction, fatigue/barriers, and improvement across repeated fixed tasks. This is a method proposal, not evidence about current players.

## Unavailable research

No project-specific interviews, observations, analytics, demographic data, accessibility study, recruitment definition, or audience validation was supplied or performed. Child-directed status, privacy regime, geographic market, language, and monetization are also unavailable and should not be inferred.

## Project consequence

- D-012 remains pending and OQ-008 remains open.
- R-015 cannot pass because T-015 lacks representative participants and approved outcomes.
- R-018 cannot pass as a settled input baseline until OQ-002, OQ-003, and OQ-004 close.
- No delivery or brand document may name a target audience as fact.
