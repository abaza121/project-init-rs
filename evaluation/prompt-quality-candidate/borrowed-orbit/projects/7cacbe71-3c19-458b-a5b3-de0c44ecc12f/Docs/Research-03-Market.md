# Research 03 — Market

## Research question

What external market or comparable-product evidence constrains the prototype?

## Result

**Research unavailable.** The supplied evidence set contains no market-size, demand, genre-performance, discoverability, pricing, monetization, retention, competitor, comparable-product, trademark, store-policy, or distribution-channel evidence. No web market research was performed in this documentation session.

The brief establishes a browser prototype and a movement-learning objective; it does not establish a commercial audience, business model, launch geography, platform channel, or success metric. This package therefore makes no market claim, ranking, competitor comparison, or commercial recommendation.

## Inferences not adopted

It would be possible to describe other one-button or gravity games from memory, but doing so would create uncited and potentially stale claims. None are included. Likewise, the name “Borrowed Orbit” is a supplied project identity (FACT-001), not evidence of trademark availability.

## Research needed before a market decision

If commercial scope is later authorized, define the decision first, then collect primary or directly attributable evidence for:

- intended players and unmet need, linked to OQ-008;
- named comparator mechanics and positioning criteria;
- browser distribution/hosting constraints;
- commercial model, geography, privacy/age scope, and success measures;
- name/domain/trademark clearance by appropriately qualified reviewers.

These are future research topics, not current requirements. No evidence record is created because no claim is retained.
