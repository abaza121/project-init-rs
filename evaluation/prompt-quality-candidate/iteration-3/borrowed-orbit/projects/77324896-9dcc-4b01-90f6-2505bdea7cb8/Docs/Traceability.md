# Relationship Guide

Authoritative relationship edges live inline on the unique canonical definition records in their owning files. This guide does not reconstruct an identifier ledger, repeat canonical identifiers, or substitute navigation for semantic links.

## Ownership Map

- Exact brief wording, normalized product obligations, and pass/fail observations live in [product commitments](Requirements.md).
- Provisional inferences and brief constraints live in [working bounds](Assumptions.md).
- Supplied answers and still-pending stakeholder choices live in [pending choices](OpenQuestions.md).
- Consequential choices, statuses, provenance, and rationale live in [technical design](TechnicalArchitecture.md).
- Externally attributable claims live only in the five topical files: [audience and evaluation](Research-01-Audience.md), [interaction and comprehension](Research-02-Experience.md), [comparable product landscape](Research-03-Market.md), [physics and browser timing](Research-04-Technology.md), and [compatibility, generation, and delivery](Research-05-Delivery.md).

## Reading Method

Start with the product commitment that owns a brief clause. Read its inline pass/fail observation, then follow the inline relationship from the relevant consequential choice or constraint. When a choice cites external material, follow the corresponding topical-file line and its direct link. When a choice is open, follow the supplied answer and pending stakeholder question in their owning register.

## Boundary

Mission, visual identity, strategic notes, the creative prompt, activity log, and audit report are explanatory views. They do not create authority or relationship edges.
