# Research 02 — Experience

## Research boundary

This research constrains how unresolved difficulty and accessibility choices may be evaluated. It does not adopt options. Product authority remains in SC-019, GOV-001, GOV-002, OQ-001, and OQ-002.

## Canonical evidence records

### EVD-001 — Difficulty is player-relative

- **Claim:** Game difficulty depends on the relationship between a player’s abilities and the barriers presented by the game, so the same barriers can produce different experiences for different players.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 108: Game difficulty options](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** It does not establish this project’s target difficulty.
- **Informs:** DEC-008.

### EVD-002 — Consult relevant players about difficulty options

- **Claim:** Microsoft recommends consulting user-research partners and relevant player communities to determine which difficulty options are appropriate for a particular game.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 108: Game difficulty options](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** Consultation is a method, not authority for a tuning value.
- **Informs:** DEC-008; OQ-001.

### EVD-003 — Input considerations

- **Claim:** Microsoft recommends remappable in-game controls, keyboard access to PC-game functions, and alternatives to simultaneous, rapid-repeat, or prolonged inputs.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107: Input](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** It does not settle remapping, menus, or assistive input scope for this prototype.
- **Informs:** DEC-009; OQ-002.

### EVD-004 — Redundant critical cues

- **Claim:** Microsoft recommends supplementing gameplay-critical color information with shapes, patterns, icons, or text and communicating critical information through an additional sensory channel.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 103: Additional channels for visual and audio cues](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** Applying a particular cue to parcels, towers, battery, multiplier, or collisions is a project-specific design inference.
- **Informs:** DEC-005; DEC-009; ASS-004.

### EVD-005 — Separate audio controls

- **Claim:** Microsoft recommends allowing separate adjustment or muting of audio categories including active effects and background or ambient audio.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 105: Audio accessibility](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/105)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** It does not define this prototype’s categories or authorize them.
- **Informs:** DEC-005; DEC-009; OQ-011.

### EVD-006 — Limit nonessential motion effects

- **Claim:** Microsoft recommends avoiding nonessential camera shake, motion blur, and similar motion effects or allowing players to disable them.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 117: Visual distractions and motion settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** A combined reduced-effects option is a project-specific recommendation, not an approved requirement.
- **Informs:** DEC-005; DEC-009.

### EVD-007 — Photosensitivity safeguards

- **Claim:** Microsoft recommends testing games for photosensitive-seizure triggers, avoiding hazardous content, and preferring elimination of hazardous content over reliance on warnings.
- **Source:** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 118: Photosensitivity](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/118)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; platform-holder guidance.
- **Limitation:** Determining whether a specific effect is hazardous requires appropriate testing; this record is not such a test.
- **Informs:** DEC-005; DEC-009.

## Evidence-based interpretation

**Evidence:** The records show that difficulty, input, cues, audio, motion, and flashing deserve deliberate evaluation.

**Recommendations, non-authoritative:** Prototype parameters behind configuration; avoid depending on color alone in exploratory mocks; keep nonessential shake and flashes removable; and preserve keyboard operation while OQ-002 remains open. None of those recommendations closes an accessibility decision.

No representative playtest, accessibility review, photosensitivity test, cue-recognition test, or approved target difficulty exists. Difficulty and accessibility remain deferred.
