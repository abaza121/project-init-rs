# SWOT

## Scope and method

This is a prototype-initiation SWOT, not market diligence. Each item is labeled by epistemic type. Brief-derived items cite source or requirement IDs; research-informed items cite canonical evidence IDs; all forward-looking judgments are inference.

## Strengths

| ID | Observation | Type | Trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| STR-001 | One action—movement—simultaneously powers the city and creates future collision geometry, giving the core mechanic a clear internal relationship. | Brief-derived fact | SC-005; SC-006; SC-007 |
| STR-002 | The run has a compact collection, matching-delivery, multiplier, collision, and battery structure. | Brief-derived fact | SC-008; SC-009; SC-010; SC-011; SC-012 |
| STR-003 | Simple shapes and no detailed-art dependency constrain prototype content scope. | Brief-derived constraint | SC-014; SC-016; CON-001 |
| STR-004 | Keyboard input and a single screen keep the initial interaction surface bounded. | Brief-derived fact | SC-002; SC-013 |

## Weaknesses

| ID | Observation | Type | Trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| WEAK-001 | Core tuning cannot be accepted because difficulty, “about two minutes,” close-pass values, and collision semantics are unresolved. | Register fact | OQ-001; OQ-006; OQ-009; OQ-010 |
| WEAK-002 | Audience and broader success criteria are absent, so representative validation and outcome claims are blocked. | Register fact | OQ-004; OQ-005; DEC-011; DEC-012 |
| WEAK-003 | The phrase “desktop and browser” does not define packaging or a compatibility matrix. | Register fact | OQ-008; DEC-013 |
| WEAK-004 | Strong audio and neon presentation create material cue, motion, and flashing questions while accessibility scope remains undecided. | Evidence-informed constraint | DEC-005; DEC-009; EVD-004; EVD-005; EVD-006; EVD-007 |

## Opportunities

| ID | Observation | Type | Trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPP-001 | A deterministic rules layer could make time, collision, delivery, and score scenarios cheaply reproducible while tuning remains configurable. | Technical inference | DEC-007 |
| OPP-002 | Redundant color and shape prototypes could improve match readability without requiring detailed art, if the stakeholder authorizes that direction. | Recommendation, non-authoritative | ASS-004; EVD-004; OQ-002 |
| OPP-003 | A web-first vertical slice could test the complete loop before native packaging is selected. | Technical inference | ASS-005; DEC-006; DEC-013 |
| OPP-004 | Audience discovery could turn difficulty and onboarding debates into questions for explicitly recruited likely players after stakeholder authorization. | Research recommendation | EVD-002; EVD-009; EVD-010; OQ-001; OQ-004 |

## Threats

| ID | Observation | Type | Trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| THR-001 | Prematurely hard-coding inferred timing or collision values could create false acceptance and expensive retuning. | Delivery inference | ASS-001; ASS-003; ASS-006; ASS-007 |
| THR-002 | Renderer-specific glow effects could produce inconsistent visuals unless a deliberate fallback is verified. | Technology inference | EVD-016; DEC-006 |
| THR-003 | Browser audio and hidden-tab behavior could break feedback or timing if interaction and visibility transitions are not designed and tested. | Technology inference | EVD-017; EVD-018; DEC-006 |
| THR-004 | Progression added without purpose or audience authority could expand persistence and balance scope without evidence of fit. | Product inference | DEC-010; OQ-003; ASS-008 |
| THR-005 | Market, trademark, store, pricing, and demand information is unavailable, so any commercial or launch commitment would be unsupported. | Research gap | See the unavailable-research section in [Research-03-Market.md](Research-03-Market.md). |

## Response priorities

1. Close or explicitly authorize bounded experiments for OQ-006, OQ-008, OQ-009, and OQ-010 before vertical-slice acceptance.
2. Keep tuning in configuration and rules independent of rendering under DEC-007.
3. Do not claim representative usability until OQ-004 and OQ-005 are resolved.
4. Treat OPP and THR items as planning inputs, never as stakeholder requirements.
