# Research 01 — Audience

## Research boundary

The intended audience is not specified. This file does not invent a persona or convert general user-research guidance into an audience decision. It supplies evidence for DEC-011 and a discovery approach for OQ-004.

## Canonical evidence records

### EVD-009 — Research likely users when they are unclear

- **Claim:** When a new product or service lacks a clear view of likely users, GOV.UK guidance recommends prioritizing research into likely users and creating personas or profiles from what is learned.
- **Source:** [Plan user research for your service — GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/plan-user-research-for-your-service)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; government service-design guidance and a primary publisher for its own guidance.
- **Limitation:** The guidance is not game-specific and cannot select an age, skill level, arcade familiarity, or access profile for this project.
- **Informs:** DEC-011; OQ-004.

### EVD-010 — Recruit actual or likely users against criteria

- **Claim:** GOV.UK guidance states that research participants should be actual or likely users and recruitment criteria should clearly specify the people needed.
- **Source:** [Finding participants for user research — GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/find-user-research-participants)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; government service-design guidance and a primary publisher for its own guidance.
- **Limitation:** It supports a recruitment method, not a project-specific participant definition or sample size.
- **Informs:** DEC-011; OQ-004.

## Evidence-based interpretation

**Evidence:** EVD-009 and EVD-010 support conducting audience discovery with explicit criteria.

**Inference, non-authoritative:** A first discovery round could contrast people with low, moderate, and high familiarity with precision arcade games, but those categories and any sample size require authorization. Do not use assumed age or genre familiarity in difficulty, onboarding, or accessibility acceptance.
