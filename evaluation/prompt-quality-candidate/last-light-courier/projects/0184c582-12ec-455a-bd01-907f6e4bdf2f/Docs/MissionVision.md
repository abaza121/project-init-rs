# Mission and Vision

## Status and authority

Everything labeled “Proposed” in this file is a product interpretation under ASS-011, not a verbatim stakeholder statement. It requires OQ-012 approval. The authoritative brief clauses remain the individually listed SC records in [Requirements.md](Requirements.md).

## Proposed mission

Create a fast, readable arcade run in which every movement is both delivery route and lasting consequence: the player lights a blacked-out city, builds the obstacle field they must survive, and earns score through planning and precision.

**Trace:** SC-002; SC-005; SC-006; SC-007; SC-009; SC-010; SC-017.  
**Status:** Proposed, non-authoritative.

## Proposed vision

Make Last Light Courier feel immediately understandable and endlessly tense within a single screen: simple shapes and decisive sound reveal a city coming alive while the player’s own luminous history closes in.

**Trace:** SC-001; SC-002; SC-004; SC-005; SC-007; SC-014; SC-015; SC-018.  
**Status:** Proposed, non-authoritative.

## Proposed experience promise

“Light the city. Read the route. Survive what you draw.”

This is draft brand copy, not source language. It captures the trail-power-maze relationship in SC-005, SC-006, and SC-007.

## Authorized design principles

These are direct operational readings of the brief, not new feature authority.

| Principle | Operational meaning | Trace |
|---|---|---|
| Consequence in every move | Movement lays persistent power and collision geometry. | REQ-005; REQ-006; REQ-007 |
| Readable at arcade speed | Critical objects and state must remain legible on one screen. | REQ-002; REQ-014; REQ-015 |
| Risk has a visible payoff | Close, collision-free passes preserve the multiplier. | REQ-011; REQ-017 |
| Short route, complete arc | Collection, matching delivery, scoring, and end state fit the battery-bounded run. | REQ-008; REQ-009; REQ-010; REQ-012 |
| Learn by immediate signals | Required onboarding is extremely short and play events have strong feedback. | REQ-015; REQ-018 |
| Prototype the system, not illustration | Geometry and effects carry the experience without detailed art. | REQ-014; REQ-016 |

## Boundaries

This mission and vision do not settle audience, difficulty, accessibility options, progression, business model, story depth, or success thresholds. The relevant unresolved records are OQ-001, OQ-002, OQ-003, OQ-004, OQ-005, and OQ-012.
