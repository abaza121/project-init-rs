## Authority snapshot

**Accepted from the brief:** name, single-screen 2D arcade form, courier and blackout premise, persistent glowing solid trails, player-built maze, approximately two-minute battery run, parcel-to-matching-tower loop, close-pass multiplier, keyboard controls, simple neon shapes, strong audio, no detailed art, route-planning and precision goals, and very short onboarding.

**Still unresolved:** difficulty, accessibility scope, progression, audience, broader success criteria, duration tolerance, onboarding threshold, delivery targets, movement and collision tuning, audio controls, and approval of proposed identity language. The canonical details are the individual OQ records in [OpenQuestions.md](OpenQuestions.md).
