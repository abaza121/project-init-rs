# Brand Prompt

## Use and authority

This prompt is for generating **concept exploration only**. It must not be presented as approved brand identity, final game art, accessible implementation, or gameplay behavior. Its inputs come from SC-001, SC-004, SC-005, SC-006, SC-007, SC-014, SC-015, SC-016, ASS-010, and the proposed system in [VisualIdentity.md](VisualIdentity.md).

## Primary prompt

Create a compact visual identity concept board for a 2D arcade game titled “Last Light Courier.” The scene is a blacked-out geometric city viewed as one fixed playfield. A tiny courier ship leaves a continuous luminous trail; that trail powers nearby city lines while also reading as a hard obstacle and a maze the player created. Use simple neon geometry, deep negative space, crisp collision edges, and minimal production detail. Show:

1. a scalable wordmark and one-color icon;
2. a tiny chevron-like courier, parcel glyph families, matching tower frames, trail core and halo, battery, and multiplier;
3. a restrained palette with dark navy-black, white, cyan, magenta, amber, lime, and fault coral;
4. examples at title-screen, gameplay, and favicon scale;
5. color-independent parcel/tower matching through repeated shapes or icons;
6. a powered-versus-unpowered city state without detailed illustration.

Tone: urgent, precise, hopeful under pressure, and legible at arcade speed. Prefer vector-like forms and stable UI over painterly texture. Make power and danger feel like the same line.

## Negative prompt

No detailed spaceship painting, photorealism, 3D render, character portrait, busy cyberpunk signage, dense lore panels, illegible glowing body text, glow-only collision boundaries, color-only matching, full-screen strobe, excessive camera shake, motion blur, ornate HUD chrome, mobile touch controls, gamepad-only cues, invented difficulty modes, progression systems, audience claims, awards, store badges, pricing, platform logos, or claims of accessibility compliance.

## Output specification

- One concept board at 16:9 plus isolated SVG-ready mark studies.
- Flat colors and transparent-background component studies.
- Each match family shown in grayscale to test shape redundancy.
- Critical HUD at representative small size without glow.
- Label all choices “proposed.”
- Do not include copyrighted characters, third-party logos, or unlicensed typeface claims.

## Review checklist

- Does the trail read simultaneously as power and collision geometry?
- Is the courier clearly smaller than towers?
- Are matching pairs identifiable without color?
- Do core forms remain legible without glow?
- Is detailed art unnecessary?
- Does the board avoid implying unresolved difficulty, accessibility scope, or progression?
- Can the icon survive one-color favicon scale?

Concept approval is OQ-012. Accessibility selection remains OQ-002.
