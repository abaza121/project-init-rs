# Research 03 — Market

## Research boundary

No market-positioning, pricing, launch, monetization, competitor-set, or commercial-success question was authorized in the brief. Research is therefore intentionally narrow and is not used to invent a market strategy.

## Canonical evidence record

### EVD-008 — Player motives vary; achievements are game-specific features

- **Claim:** A 2025 peer-reviewed study developed a nine-factor model from 1,648 open-ended gaming-motive descriptions and validated it with separate UK and US samples of 600 participants each; the authors distinguish general motives from context-dependent game features such as achievements.
- **Source:** [Validating Motives of Autonomous Players (MAP) inventory — User Modeling and User-Adapted Interaction](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11257-025-09431-7)
- **Provenance:** Imported from the supplied project snapshot; retrieved 2026-08-30.
- **Reliability:** High; peer-reviewed primary research.
- **Limitation:** The sample does not identify Last Light Courier’s audience, demand, willingness to pay, competitor set, or preferred progression. Achievements are an example in the paper, not a recommendation for this game.
- **Informs:** DEC-010; OQ-003.

## Evidence-based interpretation

**Evidence:** EVD-008 supports treating progression features as context-dependent rather than inevitable.

**Inference, non-authoritative:** A short score-focused loop does not by itself justify achievements, unlocks, power growth, or no metaprogression. OQ-003 must define purpose and gameplay-effect boundaries first.

## Research unavailable by design

The following were not researched and must not be inferred from this package: addressable market, comparable games, price, monetization, store/platform strategy, demand, retention benchmark, publishing route, brand conflicts, or trademark availability. If commercial planning becomes in scope, create a separately authorized research brief with territories, platforms, business model, comparison criteria, and decision owner.
