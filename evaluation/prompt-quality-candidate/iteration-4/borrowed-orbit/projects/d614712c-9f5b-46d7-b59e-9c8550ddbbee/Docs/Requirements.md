# Product Contract

## Requirements

REQ-001: The prototype shall deliver “a minimalist one-button browser game,” using one primary gameplay action in a browser; exact brief wording: “a minimalist one-button browser game”.
REQ-002: The prototype shall make “a small satellite survives by borrowing gravity from objects moving across the screen” the core survival interaction; exact brief wording: “a small satellite survives by borrowing gravity from objects moving across the screen”.
REQ-003: While the primary action is held, the prototype shall implement “Holding the button attaches the satellite to the nearest planet or piece of debris”; exact brief wording: “Holding the button attaches the satellite to the nearest planet or piece of debris”.
REQ-004: On release, the prototype shall ensure “releasing it preserves momentum and sends the player into a new arc”; exact brief wording: “releasing it preserves momentum and sends the player into a new arc”.
REQ-005: The prototype shall let the player “collect data fragments”; exact brief wording: “collect data fragments”.
REQ-006: The prototype shall let the player “skim hazards for bonus points”; exact brief wording: “skim hazards for bonus points”.
REQ-007: The prototype shall let the player “keep an orbit multiplier alive”; exact brief wording: “keep an orbit multiplier alive”.
REQ-008: The prototype shall make “the available objects drift toward a collapsing star” during play; exact brief wording: “the available objects drift toward a collapsing star”.
REQ-009: Every run shall satisfy “Runs should take less than three minutes”; exact brief wording: “Runs should take less than three minutes”.
REQ-010: The ended-run flow shall “restart instantly” through the primary action; exact brief wording: “restart instantly”.
REQ-011: The playable interaction shall “be understandable from animation and sound instead of text”; exact brief wording: “be understandable from animation and sound instead of text”.
REQ-012: The presentation shall use “sparse geometric space art that a solo developer could produce”; exact brief wording: “sparse geometric space art that a solo developer could produce”.
REQ-013: The prototype shall prove “switching gravity anchors creates skillful, predictable movement on one screen”; exact brief wording: “The prototype should prove that switching gravity anchors creates skillful, predictable movement on one screen.”
REQ-014: The project shall keep four choices unapproved until stakeholder decisions are supplied; exact brief wording: “I have not chosen the exact physics model, input accessibility features, target frame rate on low-end devices, or how much randomness each run should contain.”

## Acceptance Criteria

AC-001: Automated input inspection observes that press, hold, release, and ended-run restart use one primary gameplay action, with no second gameplay action required; pass if all scripted actions complete and fail otherwise supports REQ-001 because the one primary gameplay action objectively tests the one-button browser game.
AC-002: In a 10-second instrumented scenario with one moving gravity object, the satellite records non-zero anchor-directed acceleration while held and can remain alive through the scenario; pass if both observations occur and fail otherwise supports REQ-002 because gravity-object acceleration and satellite survival directly test borrowing gravity from objects moving across the screen.
AC-003: For every case in a fixture containing at least one planet and one piece of debris, pressing selects the eligible object with the smallest measured center distance at the press instant and keeps that selected anchor until release; pass on exact selection and lock in every case and fail otherwise supports REQ-003 because nearest planet or piece of debris attachment is inspected at hold onset.
AC-004: A release test samples satellite world-space velocity immediately before and after release and finds each component unchanged within 0.000001 simulation units per second, then observes continuous travel into the subsequent trajectory; pass if both velocity continuity and trajectory continuity hold and fail otherwise supports REQ-004 because preserved momentum and the new arc are tested at release.
AC-005: In a fixture with one data fragment, contact removes that fragment and increments the run fragment count by exactly one; pass if both state changes occur once and fail otherwise supports REQ-005 because fragment contact objectively tests collect data fragments.
AC-006: In fixtures for the configured hazard skim boundary and collision boundary, entering only the skim band adds a positive bonus while entering the collision boundary does not add that skim bonus; pass if both outcomes occur and fail otherwise supports REQ-006 because the distinct skim hazard observation tests bonus points without conflating collision.
AC-007: A scripted valid anchor chain preserves a multiplier greater than one through every configured continuation window, while a scripted broken chain resets it to the configured base value; pass if both exact state transitions occur and fail otherwise supports REQ-007 because multiplier preservation and reset test keeping an orbit multiplier alive.
AC-008: For each available planet or debris object in a 10-second drift fixture, logged distance to the collapsing star is smaller at the end than at the start; pass if every available object closes distance and fail otherwise supports REQ-008 because star-distance change objectively tests objects drifting toward a collapsing star.
AC-009: Across 100 automated runs that are not ended earlier by failure, the terminal event occurs before 180000 milliseconds of run time; pass if every measured run is below the threshold and fail otherwise supports REQ-009 because the strict 180000-millisecond boundary tests less than three minutes.
AC-010: Across 30 ended-run trials, primary-action activation reaches a fresh playable state within 500 milliseconds and resets score, timer, satellite, anchors, fragments, hazards, and multiplier; pass if every trial meets both timing and clean-state checks and fail otherwise supports REQ-010 because the provisional 500-millisecond threshold makes restart instantly observable.
AC-011: In a provisional five-person first-use trial with instructional text disabled, at least four participants independently attach, release, and reattach within 60 seconds while animation and sound indicate each transition; pass at four or more successes and fail otherwise supports REQ-011 because demonstrated control use without text tests understanding from animation and sound.
AC-012: Asset inspection finds that all gameplay visuals are composed from geometric primitives, flat fills, strokes, particles, or generated gradients and that the prototype art log remains within five solo-developer days; pass if both inventory and time cap hold and fail otherwise supports REQ-012 because primitive composition and a provisional solo art budget test sparse geometric space art a solo developer could produce.
AC-013: Replaying the same initial state and timestamped input sequence 100 times on one supported build yields satellite positions within 0.001 simulation units at every fixed-step sample and never changes viewport or camera; pass if all replays and the one-screen inspection comply and fail otherwise supports REQ-013 because deterministic gravity-anchor movement and one screen objectively test predictable movement.
AC-014: In the same authored challenge attempted five times by each of at least five provisional evaluators, at least four evaluators improve their successful gate count from their first attempt to their best later attempt; pass at four or more improvements and fail otherwise supports REQ-013 because repeatable improvement in gravity-anchor switching is the provisional observation for skillful movement.
AC-015: Register inspection finds no approved numeric physics tuning, accessibility scope, low-end frame-rate threshold, or run-randomness amount and finds one unresolved question for each; pass if all four remain visibly pending and fail otherwise supports REQ-014 because the four unchosen project choices remain unapproved rather than being invented.

## Constraints

CON-001: “Runs should take less than three minutes” is a strict run-duration constraint supports REQ-009 because both records constrain every run to less than three minutes.
CON-002: “be understandable from animation and sound instead of text” constrains gameplay instruction to non-text cues supports REQ-011 because both records require understanding from animation and sound instead of text.
CON-003: “sparse geometric space art that a solo developer could produce” constrains visual asset scope supports REQ-012 because both records limit the art to sparse geometric work feasible for a solo developer.
CON-004: “switching gravity anchors creates skillful, predictable movement on one screen” constrains the prototype to one play screen supports REQ-013 because both records require gravity-anchor proof on one screen.
CON-005: Stakeholder authority constrains the physics model, accessibility scope, low-end frame-rate target, randomness amount, and intended audience from becoming approved through research alone supports REQ-014 because the unchosen choices require stakeholder approval.
