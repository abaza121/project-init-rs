# Purpose and Direction

## Mission

Create a minimalist one-button browser game in which a small satellite survives by borrowing gravity from moving planets and debris: hold to attach to the nearest eligible object, release while preserving momentum, collect data fragments, skim hazards for bonus points, and sustain an orbit multiplier as the field drifts toward a collapsing star.

## Vision

Deliver a sparse geometric space experience whose movement reads through animation and sound, rewards deliberate timing, fits a solo developer’s production reality, and turns every sub-three-minute run into an immediately restartable attempt at a cleaner orbital line.

## Prototype proof

The prototype exists to determine whether switching gravity anchors creates skillful, predictable movement on one screen. Success means deterministic motion is technically inspectable and provisional evaluators can learn to improve at an authored challenge; it does not yet establish audience fit, accessibility completeness, market demand, or final tuning.

## Principles

- One action, deep consequences.
- Motion communicates before words do.
- Failure returns quickly to play.
- Geometry carries both style and state.
- Prototype measurements inform owner decisions; they do not replace them.
