# Creative Brief

Use this as a proposed exploration prompt, not as approved brand artwork or an expansion of gameplay scope.

Design a one-screen visual and audio identity for **Borrowed Orbit**, a minimalist one-button browser game about a small satellite surviving by borrowing gravity from moving planets and debris.

Show a warm-ivory diamond satellite cutting a continuous arc through near-black indigo space. A thin violet-blue gravity tether connects it to one circular planet while angular debris, cyan data fragments, coral hazard outlines, and a distant amber-red collapsing star drift inward. Use generous negative space, crisp geometric primitives, flat fills, restrained gradients, and a small number of readable particles. The composition should communicate hold-to-attach, release-with-momentum, collection, near-miss danger, and multiplier rhythm through position, trails, pulses, and contrast rather than instructional text.

The feeling is precise, lonely, urgent, and learnable. Keep every element feasible for one developer to draw or generate. Avoid photorealism, dense star fields, painted nebulae, complex spacecraft detail, text overlays, HUD clutter, perspective camera movement, visual noise, and imagery that suggests multiple simultaneous gravity anchors.

For sound, pair attachment with a soft tonal lock, held gravity with a restrained rising harmonic, release with a clean breath-like cut, data with a bright two-note ping, skims with a sharp tangent spark, multiplier continuation with an accelerating pulse, and failure with a short inward collapse. Sound must reinforce animation but must not be the sole carrier of critical state.
