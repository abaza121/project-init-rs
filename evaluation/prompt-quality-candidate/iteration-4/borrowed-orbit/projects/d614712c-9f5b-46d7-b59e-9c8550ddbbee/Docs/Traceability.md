# Relationship Guide

Authoritative relationship edges live inline on the unique canonical definition records in their owning files. This guide deliberately does not reconstruct an identifier ledger, matrix, or summary.

Begin with the [product contract](Requirements.md) for scope, checks, and constraints. Follow its inline relationships to the [working hypotheses](Assumptions.md), [pending clarifications](OpenQuestions.md), and [technical design](TechnicalArchitecture.md). Externally attributable claims are defined only in the five inquiry files: [player fit](Research-01-Audience.md), [interaction](Research-02-Experience.md), [landscape](Research-03-Market.md), [motion and simulation](Research-04-Technology.md), and [delivery](Research-05-Delivery.md).

The [purpose statement](MissionVision.md), [project lens](SWOT.md), [visual system](VisualIdentity.md), and [creative brief](BrandPrompt.md) provide interpretation and production guidance but do not replace the canonical registers. The [session record](log-2026-08-30-10-31-30.md) and [check report](ValidationReport.md) describe process only.
