# Player Fit Inquiry

## Scope

The intended player audience is not supplied. This inquiry therefore addresses only the boundary on participant recruitment; it does not choose an audience or generalize prototype results.

## Findings

EVD-001: W3C states that participant experience for accessibility evaluation depends on the target users and cautions against generalizing one disabled participant’s input to all disabled people ([W3C WAI](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/involving-users/)) supports DEC-010 because target-user-dependent participant selection justifies deferring representative-player claims until the audience is approved.

## Interpretation

The externally supported point constrains how later evaluation may be described. The inference that convenience evaluators can still expose defects is non-authoritative, and no audience recommendation is made.

## Gaps

Primary players, age range, prior arcade-game familiarity, device habits, accessibility characteristics, and recruitment criteria are unavailable. These require stakeholder direction before representative testing.
