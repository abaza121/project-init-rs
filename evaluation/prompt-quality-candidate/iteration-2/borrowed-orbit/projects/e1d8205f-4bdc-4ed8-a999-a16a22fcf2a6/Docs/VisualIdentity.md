# Visual System

## Core character

The confirmed direction is sparse geometric space art (`REQ-013`): quiet void, high-contrast silhouettes, economical motion, and short luminous traces that explain physical state. Minimalism refers to a bounded communication system, not absent feedback.

## Shape language

- Satellite: a small diamond core with two short rectangular panels; orientation follows velocity.
- Planets: circles with one orbit ring or surface notch; scale communicates gravity influence only after the physics choice is approved.
- Debris: irregular convex polygons with slow rotation.
- Data fragments: paired chevrons or tiny broken squares with a regular pulse.
- Hazards: angular forms with an outward warning rhythm.
- Collapsing star: a dense circle with an inward radial pulse and restrained corona.

These forms are provisional applications of `ASM-001`; they can change without altering the brief.

## Provisional palette

`ASM-008` proposes deep navy `#07111F` for space, warm white `#F4F1E8` for the satellite and neutral information, cyan `#59E1E8` for attachment and fragments, amber `#FFC857` for multiplier and skim rewards, and red-orange `#FF5A47` for hazards and collapse pressure. The palette is not stakeholder-approved (`DEC-021`).

## Provisional typography

`ASM-009` proposes a system sans-serif stack with tabular numerals for score and multiplier. Type is limited to score, multiplier, and browser-level controls; it must not become instructional copy that conflicts with `REQ-012`. This typography choice is not stakeholder-approved (`DEC-022`).

## Motion and sound grammar

- Available anchor: a subtle radial breath.
- Selected anchor: cyan line and one rising tone.
- Attached: curved trajectory trace and stable tonal bed.
- Release: trace detaches cleanly with a short upward transient.
- Fragment collection: inward snap and bright two-note cue.
- Valid skim: amber edge flash and compressed click.
- Multiplier expiry: shrinking ring and falling interval.
- Terminal state: starward contraction followed by a restart-ready pulse.

The event set operationalizes `REQ-012`; synthesized implementation is only proposed under `ASM-007` and `DEC-020`.

## Production guardrail

Every visual element should be expressible as a primitive, parameter, shader-free blend, or reusable procedural animation. Any proposed bespoke illustration or frame-by-frame sequence must demonstrate why primitives cannot satisfy the same communication need.
