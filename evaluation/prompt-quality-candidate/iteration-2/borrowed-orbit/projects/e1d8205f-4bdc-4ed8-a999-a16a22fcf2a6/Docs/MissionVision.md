# Purpose and Direction

## Mission

Create a fast, legible browser game in which one held-and-released action lets players borrow gravity, preserve momentum, collect fragments, take scoring risks, and sustain an orbit multiplier in runs shorter than three minutes (`REQ-002` through `REQ-010`).

## Vision

Deliver a one-screen prototype whose motion can be learned, anticipated, and mastered through animation and sound, using a sparse geometric production language achievable by one developer (`REQ-012` through `REQ-015`).

## Prototype outcome

The prototype succeeds when objective tests demonstrate the movement promise, short loop, immediate replay, non-textual comprehension, and solo-producible presentation. It does not need to prove metagame progression, monetization, accounts, narrative content, or online services because none are authorized by the brief.

## Design principles

- One action, multiple consequences: attachment timing should drive motion, collection, risk, and multiplier continuity.
- Readable causality: animation and sound should expose selection, attachment, release, skim, collection, danger, and failure.
- Short recovery loop: failure should move directly into another attempt.
- Mastery over mystery: any eventual randomness must preserve learnable cause and effect; `OQ-004` remains open.
- Scope follows proof: additions are justified only when they help test `REQ-014` or satisfy another canonical rule.
