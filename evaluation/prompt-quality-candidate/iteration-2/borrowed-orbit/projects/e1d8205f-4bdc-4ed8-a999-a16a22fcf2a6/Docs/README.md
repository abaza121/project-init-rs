## Authority order

The supplied brief governs `REQ-*` and `CON-*` records. Supplied responses are preserved as `ANS-*` records but do not settle choices they explicitly leave open. `ASM-*` records are non-authoritative. `DEC-*` records declare whether a choice is confirmed, proposed, or deferred. `EVD-*` records may inform a decision but never replace stakeholder authority.

## Prototype snapshot

Borrowed Orbit is planned as a one-button, one-screen browser prototype about attaching a satellite to nearby moving bodies, releasing with preserved momentum, collecting fragments, skimming hazards, and sustaining a multiplier before objects collapse into a star. The physics model, input-accessibility feature set, low-end-device performance target, and run randomness remain open.
