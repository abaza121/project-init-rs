# Strategic Snapshot

## Strengths

- The same tuning action controls perception, rescue, and threat, giving the tuning, reveal, entity, and loop records a coherent interaction spine grounded directly in the brief.
- A nominal five-minute run target and score-attack form create a bounded replay unit under REQ-001 and REQ-006.
- The one-room abstract presentation in REQ-008 permits a focused first-playable experiment without requiring final art.

## Weaknesses

- Hidden information has no authorized intriguing-versus-unfair threshold; this is the explicit central risk in REQ-009 and OQ-003.
- Score priorities are not defined, so the displayed total cannot yet communicate a stakeholder-approved incentive hierarchy; see OQ-002.
- Accessibility scope, audience, duration tolerance, success criteria, exclusions, technology, and telemetry remain open in their individually identified OQ records.

## Opportunities

- Non-authoritative inference under ASM-004: deterministic scenarios could make frequency reveals, hearing transitions, rescue timing, and score components cheap to regression-test.
- Candidate method under DEC-016: once stakeholders supply weights, iterative playtests could observe whether score-seeking behaviour matches the intended risk and reward hierarchy.
- Candidate methods under DEC-018 and DEC-019: once stakeholders supply the fairness boundary, objective performance and subjective response measures could examine the core hidden-information risk.

## Threats

- If a frequency change causes harm without a learnable signal, the central risk in REQ-009 may dominate the experience; the acceptable boundary remains OQ-003.
- If sound carries gameplay-critical information before OQ-001 is resolved, the first playable may embody an unapproved accessibility choice; DEC-010 prevents that choice from being mislabeled as settled.
- Non-authoritative inference under ASM-005: collecting playtest telemetry without an answer to OQ-009 could create privacy and consent obligations not covered by this package.

## Response

Build the minimum stateful loop, keep raw score components separate, preserve deterministic hooks, and obtain stakeholder answers before selecting weights, cutoffs, exclusions, audience claims, accessibility features, or telemetry policy. This response is a provisional sequencing inference, not a commitment of scope or schedule.
