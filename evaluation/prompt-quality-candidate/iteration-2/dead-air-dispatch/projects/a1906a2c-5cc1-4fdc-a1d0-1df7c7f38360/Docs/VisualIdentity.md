# Identity System

## Status

The identity is a provisional first-playable direction governed by DEC-023, DEC-024, DEC-025, and DEC-026. It is not stakeholder-approved final branding and does not settle OQ-001 accessibility treatments.

## Core Idea

“Civil defence broadcast interrupted by an impossible weather system.” The player should read the station as a practical instrument under strain rather than a decorative retro radio. Shapes should imply function first: dial, band, carrier wave, route, contact, antenna, and interference.

## Mark

Use a compact circular tuning dial crossed by one broken waveform. The break suggests dead air; a small off-centre beacon point suggests a caller. Under DEC-025, the mark has a provisional one-colour silhouette check at 32 px; this is a design target, not an externally validated accessibility rule.

## Palette

- `Storm Black` — `#090B10`, primary field.
- `Receiver Bone` — `#DDD7C8`, labels and neutral signal.
- `Carrier Cyan` — `#57D6D2`, tuned information.
- `Warning Amber` — `#F0A13A`, dangerous-band indication.
- `Interference Magenta` — `#C54E8B`, hostile contamination.

Colour roles are provisional. No gameplay meaning may rely on this palette as a settled accessibility solution; candidate redundant shape, motion, text, or pattern channels remain subject to OQ-001 and DEC-013.

## Type

Under DEC-026, use a narrow uppercase display face for call signs and headings, paired with a legible monospaced or humanist sans-serif face for frequency values, timers, captions if later authorized, and diagnostic text. Font licensing, exact families, glyph coverage, and minimum sizes remain part of OQ-008 and OQ-001.

## Shape and Motion

Clean carrier waves indicate stable signal; doubled or broken contours indicate uncertainty; inward-moving arcs indicate station approach; route geometry is thin and continuous; hazards use discontinuous blocks. Static should arrive in controlled layers tied to system state. None of these cues is approved as an accessibility parity rule.

## Sound Character

Layer dry radio clicks, narrow-band voice texture, wind pressure, carrier tones, and hostile interference so tuning feels physical. Gameplay must not assume any no-audio equivalent until OQ-001 is answered; sound-layering here implements the permitted material in CON-001, not accessibility scope.

## Copy Voice

Use short operational phrases: call sign, instruction, bearing, condition, and loss of contact. Avoid jokes in critical moments. Supernatural language should intrude as signal corruption rather than lore exposition, following DEC-024.
