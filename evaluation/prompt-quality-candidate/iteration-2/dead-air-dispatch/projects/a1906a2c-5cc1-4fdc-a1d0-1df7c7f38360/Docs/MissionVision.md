# Purpose and Direction

## Mission

Build a compact PC score-attack experience in which operating an emergency radio during a supernatural storm turns tuning into both perception and danger. The immediate delivery focus is the complete scan-identify-hold-guide-retune loop in REQ-005, realized within the low-fidelity, one-room boundary in REQ-008.

## Vision

Make hidden information feel like a legible, player-controlled source of tension: every frequency reveals a useful slice of the world while changing what can find the station. The intended long-term player experience is a repeated contest between rescue confidence, tuning precision, dangerous-band exposure, and the need to move before interference reaches the antenna.

## Near-Term Outcome

The first playable should make every brief-derived gameplay record observable and collect only the measurements authorized after OQ-003, OQ-006, and OQ-009 are answered. It is not yet authorized to claim audience fit, accessibility completeness, a fair hidden-information threshold, or project success.

## Principles

- Tuning is a world-state choice, not a cosmetic filter; see DEC-002, DEC-003, and DEC-004.
- Rescue and threat share the same frequency system; see DEC-005.
- Score components remain inspectable before their priorities are selected; see DEC-007 and OQ-002.
- Unknowns stay visible in their canonical OQ records.
- External guidance can shape candidates but cannot replace stakeholder scope authority; see CON-004.
