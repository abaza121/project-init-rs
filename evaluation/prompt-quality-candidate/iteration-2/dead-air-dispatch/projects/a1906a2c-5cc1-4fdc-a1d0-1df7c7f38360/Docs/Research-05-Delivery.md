# Delivery and Validation

## Cited Findings
EVD-006: Criterion weights represent the relative strength of preference between criteria and must be elicited from decision stakeholders ([An Introductory Guide to Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/an-introductory-guide-to-mcda/)) supports DEC-015 because stakeholder preference over scoring weights informed the unresolved incentive-hierarchy decision.
EVD-007: An empirical game-design study found that changes to risk and reward affected player strategy and used iterative player-performance data to balance the reward structure ([Balancing Risk and Reward to Develop an Optimal Hot-Hand Game](https://www.gamestudies.org/1101/articles/williams_nesbitt_eidels_elliott)) supports DEC-016 because risk-and-reward player strategy informed the candidate scoring playtest method.
EVD-008: NASA requirements guidance says performance specifications should address timing and qualitative or performance values should have measurable, verifiable, defensible tolerances ([NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, Appendix C](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/system-engineering-handbook-appendix/)) supports DEC-020 because measurable timing tolerances informed the decision to leave the run-duration tolerance open rather than invent one.
EVD-009: UK government guidance says teams should agree in advance what evidence would justify continuing, adapting, or stopping a prototype ([Test and Learn, Magenta Book Annex](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/test-and-learn-html)) supports DEC-021 because advance prototype decision evidence informed the unresolved first-playable success boundary.
EVD-010: UK government project-management guidance says work specifically excluded from a project should be defined and agreed by interested parties, and Project Initiation Document approval includes agreement on scope and exclusions ([Guidelines for Managing Projects](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/31979/10-1257-guidelines-for-managing-projects.pdf)) supports DEC-022 because stakeholder-agreed exclusions informed the decision not to infer exclusions from the one-room first-playable scope.

## Application

EVD-006 and EVD-007 distinguish authority over score priorities from empirical tuning. EVD-008 supports measurable timing once a tolerance is supplied. EVD-009 supports defining the continue-revise-stop boundary before a decision test. EVD-010 supports obtaining agreement on exclusions; none of these claims supplies the missing project-specific value.
