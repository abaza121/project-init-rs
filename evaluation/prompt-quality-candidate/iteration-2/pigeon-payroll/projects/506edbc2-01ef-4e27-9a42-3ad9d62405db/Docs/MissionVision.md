# Purpose and Direction

## Mission

Turn a frantic cafe nuisance into a short, funny one-thumb balancing act: protect outdoor customers by herding pigeons while every breadcrumb makes the flock—and the wage deduction—worse.

## Vision

Make Pigeon Payroll instantly legible, replayable, and mischievous: a pocket arcade shift where chaos rises, consequences are comic, and the player finishes wanting one more try.

## Principles

- One gesture, two consequences: a swipe solves the immediate flock problem while creating attraction and cleanup costs.
- Read the joke at a glance: silhouettes, expressions, motion, and audio make cause and effect obvious.
- Escalate through visible events: table moves, pastry appearances, food theft, and combos build the shift rather than relying on hidden difficulty.
- Preserve playfulness: avoid framing uncertainty, failure, or score deductions as punishment.
- Prototype to learn: do not invent the audience, failure rule, success threshold, behaviour set, scoring economy, or platform.

The mission and vision summarize the canonical gameplay and presentation requirements. They do not resolve any of the six canonical OQ records.
