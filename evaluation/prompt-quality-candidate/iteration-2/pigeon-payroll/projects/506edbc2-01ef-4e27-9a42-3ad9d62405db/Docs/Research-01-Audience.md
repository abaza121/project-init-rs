# Audience Landscape

## Current Findings

EVD-001: Google Play states that an app including children in its target audience must comply with Families Policy requirements and that multiple age groups should be selected only when the app is designed for each selected group. [Manage target audience and app content settings — Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en-EN) supports DEC-001 because child-inclusive audience positioning has Google policy consequences without selecting Pigeon Payroll’s audience.
EVD-002: Apple describes its Kids category as covering apps designed specifically for children aged 11 and under, divided into the 5-and-under, 6–8, and 9–11 bands with additional Kids-app review obligations. [Categories and Discoverability — Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/categories/) supports DEC-001 because deliberate children’s audience positioning has Apple category consequences without selecting Pigeon Payroll’s audience.
EVD-003: The FTC lists subject matter, visual and audio content, animated characters, intended audience, and empirical audience composition among child-directed factors, while noting that bright colors and animated characters can also appeal to general audiences. [Complying with COPPA: Frequently Asked Questions — Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) supports DEC-001 because contextual child-directed factors show that colorful slapstick styling alone cannot select Pigeon Payroll’s audience.
