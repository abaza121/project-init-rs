# Strategic Snapshot

## Strengths

- The one-finger swipe creates an immediately explainable action loop (REQ-009).
- Breadcrumbs solve and worsen the same problem, giving the core action a built-in tradeoff (REQ-010 and REQ-011).
- A 60–90-second portrait shift has a bounded arcade cadence (REQ-012 and REQ-020).
- One cafe screen and three tables make the first build deliberately contained (REQ-021 and REQ-022).
- The cafe payroll theme gives score deductions, visual identity, and comedy a shared metaphor (DEC-007 and DEC-012).

## Weaknesses

- The audience is not selected, so age-appropriate interaction, content, store positioning, and testing participants cannot be finalized (OQ-001).
- The failure policy is not selected, so the complete shift-state model remains open (OQ-002).
- The prototype lacks a stakeholder learning goal and go/no-go threshold (OQ-003).
- The exact behaviour catalogue and scoring economy are not specified, blocking full implementation and balance work (OQ-004 and OQ-005).
- The platform and its device/performance budgets are not selected (OQ-006).

## Opportunities

- Test the unusual “help now, pay later” breadcrumb action as the first candidate gameplay risk once the stakeholder selects a learning priority.
- Use deterministic event logging to compare failure-policy and tuning variants without hard-coding an answer (ASM-003 and DEC-021).
- Let wage slips, cleanup deductions, and escalating bird theft unify interface feedback with the comedy premise.
- Expand later through cafe layouts, bird personalities, and shift modifiers only if the first prototype supplies a stakeholder-authorized reason.

## Threats

- Colorful slapstick presentation may be interpreted as child-directed even when intent is unclear; EVD-001, EVD-002, and EVD-003 constrain later positioning but do not settle it.
- Escalation can undermine the explicit playful-not-stressful constraint if readability, audio layering, or penalties become oppressive (CON-003).
- Unsupported behaviour lists or score constants could make a prototype answer a question the stakeholder never chose (CON-011).
- Premature platform commitment could create hardware, distribution-cost, or device-coverage rework (DEC-006).

## Interpretation

Strengths and weaknesses above rest on supplied scope. Opportunities and threats are planning inferences, not forecasts or stakeholder decisions; consequential versions must be promoted to explicit ASM or DEC records before implementation.
