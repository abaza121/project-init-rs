# Look and Feel

## Core Idea

“Cafe payroll meets pigeon slapstick”: an orderly vertical cafe becomes a readable stage for round birds, expressive customers, airborne crumbs, and an increasingly absurd wage statement.

## Working Palette

- Espresso navy `#24313A` for outlines and high-contrast interface frames.
- Receipt cream `#FFF4D6` for menus, score cards, and calm negative space.
- Breadcrumb gold `#F4B942` for throwable crumbs and cost feedback.
- Pigeon blue-grey `#708A9B` with petrol-neck accent `#2E8B84`.
- Pastry coral `#E96B5A` for food targets and comic incident beats.
- Wage green `#4FA66B` and deduction red `#D94C4C`, always paired with labels or icons rather than color alone.

## Shape and Type

Characters use oversized heads, compact bodies, distinct silhouettes, and two-value facial features that survive gameplay scale. Tables remain simple rounded shapes with unobstructed occupancy zones. Display lettering uses a heavy, condensed, receipt-poster feel; interface numerals use a bold tabular face so wage changes do not shift layout.

## Motion and Audio

Swipes create quick arcing crumb trails. Pigeons hop, skid, flap, and briefly squash on direction changes. Food theft lands with an exaggerated snatch-pop; combos layer escalating coos and register-bell accents. Movement returns quickly to a readable pose so increasing chaos does not erase state.

## Guardrails

This direction implements DEC-015 and DEC-016 only as a provisional prototype identity under ASM-005. It must remain readable in portrait mode, keep crucial state away from touch occlusion, avoid fear or injury cues, and be revisited after OQ-001 is resolved.
