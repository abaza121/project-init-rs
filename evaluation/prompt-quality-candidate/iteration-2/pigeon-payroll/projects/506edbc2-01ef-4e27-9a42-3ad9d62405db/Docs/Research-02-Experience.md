# Player Experience Guidance

## Current Findings

EVD-004: Microsoft’s game accessibility guidance identifies young and casual players among groups affected by difficulty barriers and recommends allowing continuation after failure without significant loss of progress. [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 108 — Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108) supports DEC-002 because recoverable game failure makes a wage-reduction experiment plausible without choosing Pigeon Payroll’s failure rule.
EVD-005: Apple’s design principles recommend forgiveness, recovery from mistakes, and avoiding recovery that costs people their time or work. [Design principles — Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles) supports DEC-002 because forgiving interaction guidance informs a recoverable failure experiment without choosing whether Pigeon Payroll failure ends a run.
