# Mission and Vision

## Mission

Create a fast, legible two-minute arcade run in which every movement is simultaneously an act of delivery, illumination, and self-imposed danger. The player should feel that route planning and precise execution—not content volume—turn a tiny courier ship into the city’s last moving source of light.

This mission is a creative synthesis of the fast arcade playfield in REQ-001, the courier fantasy in REQ-003, the trail loop in REQ-004, REQ-005, REQ-006, and REQ-007, the run loop in REQ-008, REQ-009, REQ-010, REQ-011, and REQ-012, and the mastery goal in REQ-016. It does not add a target audience, default difficulty, accessibility package, progression model, or continuation threshold.

## Vision

Last Light Courier becomes instantly understandable in motion: pick up, match, deliver, skim danger, and survive the maze drawn by your own previous decisions. A complete run leaves behind a luminous record of planning under pressure.

The vision is intentionally bounded to the initiation prototype. It does not imply a commercial roadmap or persistent progression while DEC-015 remains deferred.

## Product Promise

- Movement always changes the tactical map.
- Light is useful, dangerous, and readable.
- Score communicates planning and precision.
- Feedback is immediate enough to learn through play.
- A failed run explains itself through visible state and event sound.

## Design Principles

1. **One action, multiple consequences.** Moving advances the courier, lays power, and closes future space.
2. **Readable at arcade speed.** Geometry, contrast, state, and audio should communicate before decoration.
3. **Mastery through route memory.** The player’s own trail is the evolving level.
4. **Short run, meaningful arc.** Pickup, route choice, delivery, risk, and battery pressure must fit the 120-second provisional interpretation in ASM-002.
5. **Open choices stay open.** The default difficulty in OQ-001, accessibility scope in OQ-002, progression in OQ-003, audience in OQ-004, and continuation criteria in OQ-005 remain stakeholder-owned.

## Exclusions

The initiation prototype does not promise narrative campaigns, procedural city generation, online leaderboards, social features, monetization, persistent upgrades, final accessibility scope, or detailed illustration. These are exclusions from current scope, not permanent product decisions.
