## Clause Preservation

The exact project title is “Last Light Courier”. The exact full brief is preserved below as supplied; the exact sentence fragments embedded beside each brief-derived requirement above provide clause-level mapping without replacing this text.

> Last Light Courier
>
> I want to make a fast single-screen 2D arcade game for desktop and browser where the player pilots a tiny courier ship through a blackout. Every movement leaves behind a glowing trail that powers the city but also becomes a solid obstacle, so the player gradually builds the maze they must survive. A run should last about two minutes: collect a parcel, route it to the matching tower, preserve a score multiplier by making close passes, and avoid colliding with old trails before the battery expires. The prototype should use keyboard controls, simple neon shapes, and strong audio feedback rather than detailed art. I want the game to reward route planning and precision without needing a tutorial longer than a few seconds, but I have not decided how difficulty, accessibility options, or longer-term progression should work.
