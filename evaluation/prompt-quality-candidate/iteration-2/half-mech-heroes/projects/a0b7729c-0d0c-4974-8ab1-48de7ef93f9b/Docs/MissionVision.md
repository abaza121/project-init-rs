# Direction

## Mission

Make two people feel like one gloriously unreliable rescue machine: communicate, recover from forced role swaps, save civilians, clear a path, and squeeze more teamwork from every three-minute attempt.

## Vision

Half-Mech Heroes becomes a television-readable couch-arcade experience where shared control turns coordination into visible skill and malfunction into generous comedy.

## Product promise

The prototype proves one compact loop: two local players operate one rescue mech across one scrolling city block, using split control domains that swap on damage. It prioritises an immediate restart, legible cause and effect, playful physics, and cooperative actions over content breadth.

The mission does not settle solo play, exact audience age, primary platform, remapping scope, difficulty scaling, hazard identities, success thresholds, or multiplier formula. Those choices remain in [OpenQuestions.md](OpenQuestions.md).
