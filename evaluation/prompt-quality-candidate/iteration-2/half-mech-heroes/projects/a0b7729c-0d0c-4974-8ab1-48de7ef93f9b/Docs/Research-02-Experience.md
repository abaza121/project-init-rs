# Player Experience Context

## Findings
EVD-002: Microsoft advises allowing players to remap analog and digital controls within the game ([Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107)); EVD-002 supports DEC-023 because the input-remapping guidance informed the action-to-input boundary without settling remapping scope.
EVD-003: Microsoft describes difficulty as subjective and advises multiple difficulty options plus separately configurable core mechanics ([Xbox Accessibility Guideline 108](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108)); EVD-003 supports DEC-022 because the difficulty-options subject informed an unconfigured DifficultyPolicy without selecting a scaling model.
EVD-004: Microsoft advises that players should be able to understand current objectives and review ongoing progress ([Xbox Accessibility Guideline 109](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/109)); EVD-004 supports DEC-026 because objective and progress clarity informed the explicit visible OutcomePolicy boundary without choosing a success threshold.
EVD-005: OSHA identifies falling or flying objects and electrical hazards from downed power lines among hazards in tornado response and recovery ([OSHA Tornado Response and Recovery](https://www.osha.gov/tornado/response)); EVD-005 supports DEC-025 because the disaster-hazard categories informed a configurable hazard catalogue but do not select the game's three hazard identities.

## Interpretation

These records identify barriers, communication needs, and plausible context. They are advisory or contextual. They do not authorise prototype remapping scope, a difficulty model, an outcome rule, or the three hazards.
