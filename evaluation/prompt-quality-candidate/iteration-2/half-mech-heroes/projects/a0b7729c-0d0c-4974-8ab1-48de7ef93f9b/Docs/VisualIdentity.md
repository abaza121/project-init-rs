# Look and Feel

## Character

Half-Mech Heroes should feel sturdy at a glance and barely held together in motion. The mech is one broad, asymmetric silhouette with an oversized magnetic arm, a blunt shield, visible joints, warning lamps, and repair patches. Civilians read as a single compact human silhouette; hazards use distinct mass, motion, and warning shapes.

## Shape language

- Mech: rectangles, heavy arcs, offset shoulders, large feet, and a single dominant arm.
- Civilians: rounded head, narrow body, lifted arms while falling, and no detail needed for recognition.
- Safe elements: circles and open negative space.
- Hazards: wedges, jagged edges, dangling lines, and directional warning chevrons.
- Interface: large blocks, short labels, fixed positions, and icons paired with text where text exists.

## Palette

- Soot navy `#17212B` for backgrounds and deep structure.
- Rescue yellow `#F6C945` for the mech and cooperative affordances.
- Magnet cyan `#38D6D9` for magnetic-arm ownership and valid targets.
- Shield coral `#FF6B57` for shield ownership, damage, and immediate danger.
- Civilian cream `#FFF1C7` for the protected subject.
- Concrete grey `#74808B` for inert wreckage.

Colour is redundant with silhouette, motion, position, and iconography; it is never the sole carrier of role, hazard, or success information.

## Motion and comedy

Movement uses heavy anticipation and slightly excessive follow-through. Failures are brief mechanical reactions—misaligned panels, coughing pistons, involuntary arm recoil, warning-light stutters—whose cause appears before the joke and whose recovery leaves controls readable. Screen shake, particles, and occlusion must never mask a role swap, civilian trajectory, hazard boundary, or the remaining time.

## Typography and framing

Use a wide, industrial sans serif with open counters and few weights. Reserve condensed display lettering for the title. Compose for a landscape television with the shared mech near the lower-middle third, threats entering from readable lanes, and status information anchored to screen edges rather than attached to moving bodies. The provisional test environment is recorded in ASM-003.
