# Creative Brief

Create key art and an in-game visual direction sheet for **Half-Mech Heroes**, a local two-player couch arcade rescue game. Show one chunky, malfunctioning rescue mech operated cooperatively by two players: movement and jumping belong to one role while a magnetic arm and shield belong to the other, with a sudden damage-triggered role swap suggested through crossed colour signals and startled body language. Place the mech in one side-scrolling disaster-zone city block, catching a falling civilian while flinging wreckage clear. Emphasise teamwork, readable cause and effect, exaggerated but weighty physics, and warm slapstick mechanical failure.

Use bold asymmetric silhouettes, large simple masses, thick industrial shapes, limited surface detail, rescue yellow, soot navy, magnet cyan, shield coral, civilian cream, and concrete grey. Design for immediate recognition on a landscape television: strong value separation, large hazards, uncluttered lanes, large status blocks, redundant icons, and no colour-only meaning. Make the danger energetic but not grim; make the mech heroic through persistence rather than competence.

Avoid photorealism, military styling, gore, tiny UI, dense debris fields, delicate line art, separate player avatars, online-play imagery, a chosen exact age rating, named platforms, specific unapproved hazard identities, or any visual that implies solo play, difficulty modes, remapping scope, success thresholds, or multiplier rules have been settled.
