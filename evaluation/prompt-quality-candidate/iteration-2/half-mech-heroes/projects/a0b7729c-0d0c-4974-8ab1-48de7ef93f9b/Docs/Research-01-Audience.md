# Audience and Age Context

## Findings
EVD-001: ESRB publishes age-appropriateness rating categories and content descriptors, but those categories do not establish Half-Mech Heroes' intended exact target age ([ESRB Ratings Guide](https://www.esrb.org/ratings-guide/)); EVD-001 supports DEC-024 because the ESRB age-category subject informs the decision to preserve the exact target age as stakeholder-owned.

## Interpretation

The retained material can help evaluate content after an audience is chosen. It cannot choose an audience, recruit representative playtesters, or convert a rating category into stakeholder intent. OQ-004 therefore remains open.
