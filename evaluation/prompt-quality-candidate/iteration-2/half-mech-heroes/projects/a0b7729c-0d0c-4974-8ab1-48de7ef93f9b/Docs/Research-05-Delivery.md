# Delivery Context

## Findings
EVD-007: Valve's current Steamworks documentation states that each new app distributed on Steam requires a US$100 Steam Direct fee ([Steam Direct Fee](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee)); EVD-007 supports DEC-028 because the Steam delivery-fee subject informed the decision to block storefront delivery tasks until the primary platform is selected.
EVD-008: Microsoft characterises the Xbox Accessibility Guidelines as best practices and development guardrails, not validation of legal or compliance requirements ([Xbox Accessibility Guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/guidelines)); EVD-008 supports DEC-029 because the best-practice status informs the decision to treat accessibility guidance as advisory rather than stakeholder authority.

## Interpretation

The Steam record is a conditional delivery fact, not evidence that Steam or PC is the target. The Microsoft record sets an authority boundary, not a project-specific compliance claim. Distribution planning remains blocked by OQ-005.
