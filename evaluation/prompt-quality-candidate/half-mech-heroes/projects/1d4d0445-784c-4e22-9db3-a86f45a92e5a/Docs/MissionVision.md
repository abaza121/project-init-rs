# Mission and Vision

## Mission

Create a three-minute local two-player rescue-arcade prototype in which two people share one unreliable mech, coordinate asymmetric controls, and repeatedly turn damage-driven role swaps into skillful saves and comic failures.

This mission is a normalized summary of SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-006, SRC-008, SRC-011, and SRC-020. The exact stakeholder wording remains canonical in [Requirements.md](Requirements.md).

## Vision

Half-Mech Heroes should make the couch feel like a tiny emergency-control room: neither player is sufficient alone, every mistake changes who does what, and improved teamwork makes the same chaos both more masterful and more amusing.

The vision is design interpretation, not additional scope. It does not resolve the open platform, technology, age, solo-play, remapping, difficulty, content-identity, or success-threshold questions in [OpenQuestions.md](OpenQuestions.md).

## Product principles

| Principle | Authoritative basis | Practical meaning |
|---|---|---|
| One body, two operators | REQ-003 | Both assignments act on one mech; avoid parallel avatars. |
| Forced adaptation | REQ-006 | Damage changes roles automatically and visibly. |
| Rescue under pressure | REQ-008; REQ-011 | Catching civilians occurs inside a fixed 180-second attempt. |
| Cooperation has consequence | REQ-010; REQ-021 | Coordinated actions affect the multiplier and measured rescue outcome. |
| Readable chaos | REQ-013; REQ-014; REQ-016 | Physics and failures can be exaggerated, but state and hazards must remain interpretable. |
| Immediate replay | REQ-012 | Restart stays in the attempt flow and resets state cleanly. |

## Prototype outcome

The initiation target is evidence that the specified cooperative loop can be implemented and evaluated in one city-block content unit with one civilian archetype and exactly three mechanically distinct hazard archetypes. It is not evidence that unapproved platforms, audiences, feature exclusions, or market positioning have been selected.
