# SWOT Analysis

Status: initiation analysis. Strengths and weaknesses derive from the brief; opportunities and threats are explicitly labelled inference. This document creates no scope or market authority.

## Strengths — evidence from project brief

- Distinctive shared-body cooperation: REQ-003, REQ-004, and REQ-005 make coordination structural.
- Role swaps turn damage into a systemic relationship change rather than only lost health: REQ-006.
- A compact 180-second loop with restart support enables rapid iteration: REQ-011 and REQ-012.
- Prototype content counts are bounded: REQ-018, REQ-019, and REQ-020.
- Mastery and comedy are both explicit intended outcomes: REQ-022 and REQ-023.

## Weaknesses — current project constraints

- Core evaluation terms lack stakeholder-approved measures: OQ-010A, OQ-010B, OQ-010C, OQ-010D, and OQ-010E.
- Platform and technology are undecided, blocking concrete budgets and tooling: OQ-005 and OQ-006.
- Hazard and civilian identities are undecided, blocking final art and behaviour specifications: OQ-007 and OQ-008.
- The city-block/disaster-zone boundary is unresolved: OQ-009.
- Shared-keyboard ergonomics and controller-side interpretation require implementation validation even though support is mandatory: REQ-004, REQ-005, and REQ-017.

## Opportunities — design inference, non-authoritative

- Damage-driven swaps may turn ordinary mistakes into replayable social stories if REQ-015 and REQ-023 test positively.
- The fixed content count may permit deep interaction polish before content expansion.
- Abstract input ports in DEC-041 allow early mechanic tests while platform and engine decisions remain open.
- Readability constraints informed by EVD-EXP-001, EVD-EXP-004, and EVD-EXP-005 can improve couch play beyond minimum legibility.

## Threats — delivery inference, non-authoritative

- Role swapping on every valid damage event may become confusing or frustrating if event frequency is not tuned.
- Exaggerated physics can create nondeterministic failures that complicate scoring and restart-state verification.
- A three-minute timer may conflict with onboarding for an audience that has not been selected.
- Platform selection may introduce SDK, certification, performance, or publishing constraints after architecture work begins; EVD-MKT-002, EVD-MKT-003, and EVD-TEC-003 inform this risk.
- Comedy measures are cohort-sensitive; an unrepresentative test group may produce misleading confidence.

## Immediate risk treatments

- Use deterministic scenario fixtures for REQ-006, REQ-008, REQ-009, and REQ-010.
- Keep platform adapters and engine APIs outside the gameplay domain until OQ-005 and OQ-006 close.
- Treat every numeric qualitative gate in Assumptions.md as provisional.
- Resolve the five split success-criteria questions before claiming experience validation.
