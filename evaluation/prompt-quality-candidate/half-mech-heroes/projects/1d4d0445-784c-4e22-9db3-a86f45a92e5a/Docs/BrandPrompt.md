# Brand Generation Prompt

Use this prompt to explore—not approve—the visual identity. Any generated asset requires human review against REQ-001, REQ-014, REQ-015, and REQ-016.

## Master prompt

> Create a bold key-art and logo exploration for **Half-Mech Heroes**, a local two-player couch rescue arcade game. Show one broad, malfunctioning rescue mech operated as two mismatched control halves: piston legs and movement systems on the left, an oversized magnetic arm and shield on the right. The mech is earnest and heroic but visibly unreliable, catching a falling civilian silhouette while flinging large wreckage aside in one scrolling city-block disaster scene. Emphasize chunky, unmistakable silhouettes, exaggerated recoverable physics, strong foreground/background separation, and television-distance readability. Use rescue amber, signal cyan, shield magenta, soot navy, concrete mist, and sparing alarm red. The comedy should come from mechanical timing and pose, not humiliation or injury. Present `HALF-MECH` as two mismatched mechanical word-halves joined at the centre, with `HEROES` as a stable rescue-stencil base. High energy, family-audience-neutral, no platform branding, no age-rating badge, no interface mock-up text beyond the exact title.

## Negative prompt

> No two separate player characters; no combat framing; no gore; no civilian harm close-up; no military mech; no photorealism; no thin silhouettes; no tiny illegible labels; no colour-only role communication; no selected real-world city; no named hazard types; no specific civilian profession, age, ethnicity, or costume; no solo-play messaging; no difficulty, remapping, engine, storefront, console, or release claims; no extra subtitle or slogan.

## Review checklist

- Exact title is present and spelled correctly.
- One shared mech is unmistakable; two independent mechs are absent.
- Left movement half and right magnetic-arm/shield half are distinct without colour alone.
- The civilian remains a neutral placeholder because OQ-008 is open.
- Hazards remain abstract because OQ-007 is open.
- No target age is implied as an approved choice while OQ-004 remains open.
- Silhouette and critical action remain interpretable at reduced 1080p gameplay scale.
- Comedy comes from the mech’s recoverable malfunction rather than civilian suffering.
