# Research 05 — Delivery and Evaluation

Research date: 2026-08-30  
Scope: primary institutional sources that constrain evaluation and prototype-scope reasoning.

## Canonical evidence

### EVD-DEL-001 — Usability depends on use context; ISO does not prescribe project methods

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: ISO 9241-11:2018 frames usability as an outcome of use and states that it does not describe specific design-development or evaluation processes and methods.
- Primary source: [ISO 9241-11:2018 summary](https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html)
- Reliability: high; official standard summary. The full standard was not accessed.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30; ISO lists the standard as published and confirmed.
- Informs: DEC-035, DEC-038, DEC-039, and DEC-040 by supporting context-specific evaluation while withholding project-specific thresholds.

### EVD-DEL-002 — A block has no standard physical extent

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: The U.S. Census Bureau defines blocks by bounding features and notes that blocks vary in size and shape.
- Primary source: [U.S. Census Bureau Geography Program Glossary](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/about/glossary.html)
- Reliability: high for geographic terminology; it has no authority over fictional level design.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-024 and OQ-009 by showing that “one city block” alone does not establish a physical extent or its relationship to the disaster zone.

### EVD-DEL-003 — Alpha prototypes should target risky assumptions

- Claim type: sourced recommendation
- Claim: The GOV.UK Service Manual advises using alpha to prototype and test risky assumptions and to build enough to support a decision about moving forward.
- Primary source: [How the alpha phase works — GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-alpha-phase-works)
- Reliability: high for general government service-delivery practice; indirect for game development.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-033 and the delivery sequence. It supports narrow experimentation but does not identify an exclusion for this game.

## Evidence-based conclusion

Evaluation must be bound to approved users, goals, contexts, methods, and thresholds. Prototype discipline should focus on risky assumptions, but neither source supplies game-specific acceptance gates or stakeholder authority over exclusions.
