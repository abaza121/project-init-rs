# Research 03 — Market and Platform Context

Research date: 2026-08-30  
Scope: official platform documentation only. No sales estimates, market sizing, competitor ranking, price research, store review mining, or commercial forecast was performed.

## Canonical evidence

### EVD-MKT-001 — Steam recognises local multiplayer capabilities

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: Steam Remote Play Together is associated with games listed for Local Multiplayer, Local Co-op, or Shared/Split Screen capability.
- Primary source: [Steam Remote Play — Steamworks Documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/remoteplay?l=english)
- Reliability: high; official storefront documentation.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-002 and DEC-030. It confirms that local capability is a distinct listing concept but does not decide solo scope or Steam targeting.

### EVD-MKT-002 — Xbox publishing access has onboarding prerequisites

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: Microsoft’s ID@Xbox onboarding requires registration and concept submission/approval steps for full Xbox development and publishing access.
- Primary source: [Join ID@Xbox — Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/game-publishing/onboarding/onboarding-join-id-at-xbox)
- Reliability: high; official platform documentation.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-027 and OQ-005 as a platform-specific delivery constraint, not a reason to select or reject Xbox.

### EVD-MKT-003 — Nintendo Switch resources require developer onboarding

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: Nintendo describes developer registration and a subsequent request for Nintendo Switch information access.
- Primary source: [Nintendo Developer Portal registration](https://developer.nintendo.com/register)
- Reliability: high; official platform documentation.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-027 and OQ-005 as a platform-specific delivery constraint, not a reason to select or reject Nintendo Switch.

### EVD-MKT-004 — Single-player is a separate Steam tag

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: Steam’s tag documentation includes Singleplayer as a player-support tag.
- Primary source: [Steam Tags — Steamworks Documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/tags?l=english&language=english)
- Reliability: high; official storefront documentation.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-030 and OQ-002 by showing that solo capability is distinct from required local multiplayer capability; it does not choose inclusion or exclusion.

## Evidence-based conclusion

Official platform records establish differing capability labels and onboarding constraints. They do not identify a primary platform, target market, commercial opportunity, price, audience, or distribution plan for this project.
