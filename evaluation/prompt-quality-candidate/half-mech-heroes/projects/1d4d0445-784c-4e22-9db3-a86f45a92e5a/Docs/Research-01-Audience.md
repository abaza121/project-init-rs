# Research 01 — Audience

Research date: 2026-08-30  
Scope: primary-source checks that constrain audience discussion. No audience interviews, demographic data, or stakeholder audience decision were available.

## Canonical evidence

### EVD-AUD-001 — ESRB categories address content-based age appropriateness

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: ESRB describes its rating categories as suggestions of age appropriateness and associates them with content considerations.
- Primary source: [ESRB Ratings Guide](https://www.esrb.org/ratings-guide/)
- Reliability: high; official classification body.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-032 and OQ-004 by showing that classification evidence can inform content suitability but does not select this project’s intended audience.

### EVD-AUD-002 — PEGI separates age suitability from difficulty

- Claim type: sourced fact
- Claim: PEGI states that age ratings indicate suitability of content and do not indicate gameplay difficulty.
- Primary source: [PEGI age ratings](https://pegi.info/page/pegi-age-ratings)
- Reliability: high; official classification body.
- Checked: available 2026-08-30.
- Informs: DEC-032 and OQ-004 by preventing a content rating from being used as a proxy for player capability or target audience.

## Evidence-based conclusion

Evidence supports only an authority boundary: age-classification information cannot choose the exact intended audience or validate gameplay difficulty for Half-Mech Heroes. Target age remains stakeholder-owned in OQ-004.

## Unavailable research

- No stakeholder audience choice.
- No interviews or observational research with children, families, adult couch-co-op players, disabled players, or mixed-skill pairs.
- No jurisdiction or ratings-submission plan.
- No approved language, content-sensitivity, motor-skill, reading-level, or cognitive-load criteria.

Any audience persona created before those inputs would be invented and is therefore omitted.
